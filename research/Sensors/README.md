# Sensors

Sensor documentation for data collection at high altitudes.

## Planned Sensors

### 1. Temperature Sensors (external and internal temperature)
Measures atmospheric temperature and flight box temperature throughout the flight.


### 2. Atmospheric Pressure Sensor
Measures changes in the atmospheric pressure as the balloon increases in altitude.


### 3. Humidity Sensor
Measures relative humidity throughout the flight.


### 4. Gyroscope / Accelerometer 
Measures changes in orientation and the payload spinning on ascent and descent. Can be used with a servo to stabilize cameras.


### 5. Camera
Gets video footage of the ascent and descent.


### 6. UV Sensor
Tracks ultraviolet radiation through the atmosphere.


### 7. Geiger Counter (Radiation Sensor)
Measures changes in ionizing radiation levels throughout the flight.


### 8. Gas Sensors
Measures changes in atmospheric gas concentrations during the flight.
